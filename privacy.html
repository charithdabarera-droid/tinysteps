TinySteps Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

TinySteps ("we", "the app") is a daily life-coach app. We designed it to collect as little data as possible.

Data stored on your device

Your check-ins (energy, money worry, mood), daily "win" text, actions, reflections, custom goals and reminder times are stored only on your device. We do not upload them to our servers, and we do not sell or share them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts. We never see your payment card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Analytics and advertising

TinySteps contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Cross-device sync (future)

If we add optional cloud sync, this policy will be updated before it launches and the feature will be opt-in.

Children

TinySteps is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use "Reset data" in the Week tab, or uninstall the app, to delete all data.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/charithdabarera-droid/tinysteps.
